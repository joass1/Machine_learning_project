# Surface brief: final report deck

Scope: slide deck, Persuade mode (graders score it against a rubric). 15-40 slides, 15 min talk plus 5 min Q&A, classroom projector, built natively in Canva for hand refinement. Slide order follows the rubric phases. A source footer appears only on slides quoting data that is not the team's own findings; never on the title slide.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The photograph carries the design; type stays quiet. Refuses the corporate template, the dense text wall and any playful pitch tone.

OWN-WORLD: Full-bleed Higgsfield aviation photography, warm dusk haze, no people, long lens, grain. Ink #16171a on haze #b9b4aa; off-white #f2f1ee panel for dense slides; no accent colour. Host Grotesk, weight 400, tracking -0.035em, line-height 1.0. A hairline top bar of tiny labels (FINAL REPORT, topic, year) on every full-bleed slide.

STORY: Graders see a rigorous, calm analysis of whether synthetic survey data can be trusted, with evidence carrying each slide.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Photo edge to edge. A 70px title starts about 260px from the left in the empty sky, a 17px one-line question under it, a thin top bar, team names small at bottom left. No footer.

FORM: Photo Haze for title and section slides; an off-white left panel (520px) for figure, table and data slides. Position 1 of the user's pinned hand; seed: none (pinned by user reference, roll overridden).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
