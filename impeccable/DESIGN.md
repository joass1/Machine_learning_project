# Design record: Cleared for Satisfaction (final deck, Canva)

Direction: Photo Haze, varied. The photograph carries the design; type is quiet and large; evidence (tables, figures) sits on white cards.
Canvas 1920 x 1080. Built natively in Canva (text, shapes, photos); tables and figures are images from matplotlib (`impeccable/work/make_tables.py`, `docs/figures/`).

## Palette
Ink #16171a | Off-white #f2f1ee | Haze #dedbd4 and #b9b4aa | Navy #0f1a2b | White #ffffff | Orange accent #e8641b (on light text #c9500f, on navy text #ff8a4a). Muted text #55575b on light, #aab0ba on dark.

## Type (Canva default grotesk now; set Host Grotesk by hand)
Headlines 56-84 px regular, line-height 1.05. Body 32-38 px. Captions 24 px. Source footers and references 22 px. Title 118 px. No bold display, no italics.

## Backgrounds and rhythm (varied on purpose)
Photo full-bleed (1) | off-white whitespace (2, 7, 11, 15, 18, 24) | haze (3, 8, 14, 19) | navy (4 split with photo, 10, 16, 22) | white (5, 9, 12, 17, 23, backups) | ink (6, 13, 20, 25) | photo with dark panel (21).
Positive/negative: dark slides carry white figure cards; light slides carry dark cells (slide 8).

## Rules held
- Footer only on slides quoting data that is not our own (3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 9); never on the title; own results have none.
- No hero-metric tiles, no kicker labels, no icon rows, no gradients, no emoji.
- Hero images: three CC-licensed photos, no AI generation (impeccable/assets/photos/PROVENANCE.md).
